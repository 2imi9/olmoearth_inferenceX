dynamic_world_tile.npz

One tile of the Dynamic World expert-annotated test set, reduced to 40 m windows: the mean of each of Dynamic World's
nine published class probabilities per 4 x 4 pixel window, and the expert's class per window (-1 where under half of the
window was annotated). Tile label_dw_-71p8353625474_-16p4098607856-20190609, southern Peru, 9 June 2019.

Source: "Dynamic World Test Tiles", Zenodo record 4766508, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4766508, licensed CC BY 4.0.
Product: Brown, C. F. et al., "Dynamic World, Near real-time global 10 m land use land cover mapping", Scientific Data 9,
251 (2022). Changes made: spatial averaging to windows, float16 storage, selection of one tile.

The tile was chosen by scripts/make_demo_sample.py with a rule fixed before any tile was looked at (the lower-median tile
by error capture at a 5% review budget among the fully annotated ones); every candidate is in
exp/out/demo_sample_selection.json of the repository.
