| From (2021) | To (2024) | Windows | Share of diffs |

Most reversals are near-absent (e.g. only 5 windows went back the other way on the top pair), so the changes read as directional rather than noise.

**Where:** changes concentrate south and mid-west of center — the most in any band is columns 25–50% of the grid (32.0% of all differing windows); rows sit at ~24–28% per quarter-band. 73.0% of differing windows touch a predicted-class boundary in the 2021 map, but that counts neighbours, not whole regions flipping. All 5,165 windows are listed with margins in `compare_differing_c8589f3f12.json` (workspace).

## Which map should you believe?

Neither can be picked from these two maps alone: they are 731 days apart, so every differing window either changed on the ground or is an error in one map, and decisions/margins can't tell which. The 2021 map has the larger margin on 51.7% of differing windows — a margin is not a label, and no recorded experiment grades which side is right here.
