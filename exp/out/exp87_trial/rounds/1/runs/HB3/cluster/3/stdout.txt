### Where

- **73.0%** of differing windows touch a predicted-class boundary in the 2021 map (vs 53.8% of all windows) — so differences cluster along class edges rather than uniform noise.
- Most in the central-west columns (columns 32–63: 32.0% of all differing windows) and the south-central row band (rows 64–95: 28.2%, the highest per-band rate at 35.6%).
- First differing rows are the north edge, where woodland_forest↔montane_forest flips dominate.

### Which map should you believe?

Neither — that question is unresolvable from these runs alone. A differing window either changed on the ground (2021→2024 includes ~2 years of seasonal cycles) or is wrong in one map, and the decision scores cannot tell which. Map A happens to have the larger margin on 51.7% of differing windows, but a margin is not a label, and no recorded experiment grades which side wins here.

To settle it, each map needs a reference dated to its own year.
