**Do they agree?** Across 25 shared cells (11 of 36 dropped as no-data, over ~10,091 km² of shared extent), the correlation is **-0.0172**, 95% interval **-0.41 to 0.38**. Because the interval spans both "no relation" and a moderate one, this run cannot say whether the maps co-vary at all. Since the bands are different quantities, no difference, RMSE, or agreement fraction exists between them — those mixes of units are not meaningful.

**Where do they disagree most?** Not determinable from this run: the correlation is a single pooled number with no location, and the tool declines per-cell disagreement for two different properties. Sampling individual pixels by hand would only give you scattered point readings, not a defensible map of disagreement.

**Which should you trust?** Neither — nothing in this run names a winner. Both models were Studio fine-tunes (train/val 75/25) on separate label fields, but no metrics or ground truth were looked up for this area, and without labels no tool here can rank them.

Suggested next steps:
- Re-run the pair comparison on a denser grid (`grid=12`, up to 144 cells) to narrow the correlation interval.
- To pick a trusted map, ground-truth is required: label planned windows via the label-sample design (the `sample_number` band needs a named decision threshold first), then estimate each map's error — or supply existing ground-truth labels for me to score directly.
