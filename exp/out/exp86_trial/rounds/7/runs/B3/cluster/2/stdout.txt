**Which is right — not determinable from these maps alone.** Two reasons, measured rather than assumed:

- **Different dates.** The maps describe 2023 vs 2022 (one-year windows apart), so a differing window may simply be real ground change or seasonal cycle between the two years, not an error in either map.
- **No reference labels.** Even with labels, none were given that describe a specific date (`labels_date`), so grading is impossible. Confidence can't break the tie either: on the differing windows C2 (2022) has a higher mean logit margin (3.19 vs 2.66 for C1), but upstream measurement puts the more-confident-side-wins rate at only 51–70% of differing windows — it does not identify a winner, and declining to pick a side is the correct call.
