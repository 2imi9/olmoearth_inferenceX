## Why this ranking, and what it does not say

- **Why**: margins here are tiny compared with the map-wide median of 4.47 — the top 819 windows end at margin 0.994, i.e. orders of magnitude less confident than a typical window. On Ai2's published suite the model's own margin beat every no-model control (ensemble disagreement, feature typicality, cross-model disagreement) on all 24 scored tasks, so this is the validated ordering for where to look.
