## Which is right?

I cannot tell you — and no comparison tool can:

1. The maps describe **different periods** (2022 vs 2023), so a difference may be real ground change or seasonal effect, not an error in either map.
2. No reference/labels were provided for either date, so nothing grades either side. The 2022 map has a larger logit margin on 57.8% of differing windows, but upstream evidence shows the more confident side was only right 51–70% of the time in comparable cases — confidence does not settle it.

To pick a winner you would need labels (or a dated reference) for each map; happy to plan a labeling design (`olmoearth_plan_label_sample`) if you want to spend effort grading these.
