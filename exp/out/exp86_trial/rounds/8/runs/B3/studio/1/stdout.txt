**Key caveats from the comparison:**

- The two models output **different quantities** (a karst score vs a number), so direct difference/RMSE statistics are not computed and I won't work any out. Only whether they rise and fall together is comparable — and with correlation ≈ **-0.0172**, they effectively do not agree spatially: one going high says essentially nothing about the other.
- **Which is right: cannot be told.** No ground-truth labels were used, so this is model-vs-model agreement, not accuracy, and neither map is judged better or more accurate. A thresholded error rate would need labeled windows.
