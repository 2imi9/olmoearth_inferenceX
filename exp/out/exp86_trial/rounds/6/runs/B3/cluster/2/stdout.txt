**Where they differ**
- **3,807 windows differ** (23.2% of the area) — same model revision (`OlmoEarth-v1-FT-AWF-Base`, rev `a347b15`) in both runs.
- Differences sit mostly **on class boundaries** (78.3% of differing windows touch a boundary vs 53.9% overall).

- On those differing windows the mean logit margin is higher for C2 (3.19) than C1 (2.66) — i.e., C2 looks more confident there, but that is not evidence it is right.

**Which is right — I can't grade this, and here is why**
- The two runs describe **different time windows** (2022 vs 2023), so any differing window may simply be real land-cover change or seasonal cycle, not an error.
- No reference labels for either date were provided, so even with them, grading would need a **reference per date** (`labels_date`); decisions alone cannot separate "changed on the ground" from "one map is wrong."
- Measured evidence says the confident side is right only ~51–70% of the time on such differing sets, so confidence does not pick a winner.
