| Zone (most-confident windows) | Labels inside | Wrong | One-sided upper bound |
|---|---|---|---|
| top 15% (~2,372 windows) | 46 | 3 | ~13.9% |
| top 25% (~3,953) | 68 | 3 | ~9.5% |
| top 50% (~7,906) | 140 | 13 | ~13.2% |
| whole map (15,813) | 300 | 69 | ~26.4% |

Even the most confident 15% of the map only reaches a ~14% worst-case error bound — the observed 3/46 errors inside it are too few for the exact test to push the bound under 5%. Nothing outside any tested zone is certified either.

To get a certified zone you'd need either (a) substantially more random labels focused on the confident core (certification needs >= 45 labels per testable zone, and much more to hit alpha = 0.05 given this ~9-14% underlying error level), or (b) accepting a looser guarantee, e.g. alpha ≈ 0.14 would certify roughly the top 15-25% of the map.
