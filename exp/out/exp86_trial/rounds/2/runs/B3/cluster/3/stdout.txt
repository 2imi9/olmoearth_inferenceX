**Which is right**

I can't tell, and it would be wrong to pick a side:

- Both maps use the same model weights, so this isn't a model-vs-model contest.
- They describe different time windows (2022 imagery vs 2023 imagery), so a differing window either changed on the ground between the two years (seasonal cycles included) or one map is wrong — the predictions alone cannot separate those two causes.
- Measured upstream, even the more confident side is only right on 51–70% of differing windows, so confidence does not identify a winner here.

To resolve it you'd need reference labels dated to each map (photointerpretation or field data for 2022 vs 2023); with those I could grade the differences per date. If you just want to know how much *movement* is real versus noise, a third date for one side would let me separate steady change from reversal.
