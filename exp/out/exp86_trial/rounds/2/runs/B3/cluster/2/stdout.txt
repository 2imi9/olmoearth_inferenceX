## Which one is right — I have to decline

**Neither can be declared right.** The two maps describe different times (all of 2022 vs all of 2023), so any differing window either changed on the ground between the years (seasonal cycles included) or is wrong in one map — and the predictions alone cannot separate those. Without reference labels dated to each map, no grading is possible at all. Two measured caveats from the package:

- "Different-time" differences are expected behavior, not evidence of error.
- Even with labels absent, picking the more-confident side would win only ~51–70% of differing windows upstream — confidence doesn't identify a winner here.
