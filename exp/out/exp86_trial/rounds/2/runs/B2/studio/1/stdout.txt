Budget cuts: at ~1% of valid windows check rank 1 alone; at ~5.6% (4 windows) through rank 4; at ~9.7% (7 windows) through rank 7. Median margin across all valid windows is 0.9647, so even the "most suspect" windows here are fairly decided — nothing is truly close to the threshold.
