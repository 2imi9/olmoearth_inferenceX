## Why this ranking

- **Signal:** each window's mean top-1 minus top-2 logit margin over its 4x4-pixel pixels, with the label-fill channel left out. The model is "most unsure" where two classes score nearly equally, so those go first.
- **Measured evidence:** in the package's test suite (Ai2's published embedding suite, 24 tasks, ~6.4M graded units), this margin beat every no-model control — ensemble/cross-model disagreement, feature-space typicality, backbone-version and two-view disagreement — on all 24/24 (sign test p = 6e-08). So "check low-margin windows first" is the empirically validated policy here, not just intuition.
- **Caveats:** this orders suspicion, it does not calibrate error probability; capture at your budget is bounded by budget/error_rate, and known exception: for one flood-event task a cheap NDWI physical control ranked as well — worth considering if water edges dominate.
