Even the best zone sits around 13% upper bound — far above 5%.

For context, the same 300 random labels estimate the **whole-map error rate at 23.0%** (exact hypergeometric 95% CI 18.4% to 28.1%), over a population of 15,813 windows. That is an estimate with an interval, not a certification.

What would help: label **more windows under a new random design** (a larger budget, fixed in advance along with alpha/delta/rule) — a bigger sample is what gives the test enough power.
