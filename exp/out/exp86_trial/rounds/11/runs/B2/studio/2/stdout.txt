A budget of 5% (~4 windows) has margin cut 0.5536; 10% (~7 windows) has cut 0.7447. All 7 remaining listed windows span margins 0.297–0.745, versus the median margin of 0.9647 over all 72 windows.

Why this order: the score is treated as P(positive) for ranking only, so low-margin windows sit on both sides of the decision boundary — exactly where the model could flip with a small input change. Note the caveats:

- These numbers are **not probabilities of error**; which windows are wrong is known only once labelled. No recorded experiment grades a regression score read as a probability.
- A review set holds suspected errors by design; its error rate would overstate the map's.
