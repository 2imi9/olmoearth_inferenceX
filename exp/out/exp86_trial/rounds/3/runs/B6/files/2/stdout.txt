Key points:

- A zone needs at least **45 labels inside it** to be certifiable at alpha = 0.05, so the smallest testable zone was 15% of the map; its error upper bound (~13.9%) is still far above 5%.
- The map-wide estimate is ~23% wrong (69/300), so a 5%-error zone would require either a much smaller, more confident region than your data resolves, or the true high-confidence region just doesn't exist at that scale.
- Practical options: label **more windows** (a larger random sample certifies smaller zones), or fix a looser alpha *before* looking at results (e.g. "at most ~14% wrong") if you can live with it — but don't pick an alpha after seeing these bounds, the guarantee doesn't cover that.
