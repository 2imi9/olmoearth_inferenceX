

I ran the review-set ranking on the KarstBinary 2025 result (`419c581d`, band `sample_karst_score`, declared range [0,1]).

**How it works:** each of the 12x12 grid windows is scored as P(karst) at its cell centre; the signal is the margin |2s - 1| — distance from the 0.5 decision boundary. Lowest margin = least decided prediction = opened first. 91 of 144 windows were valid (53 no-data dropped). Note this ranks suspicion, not calibrated error probability — the score near 0.5 means "model isn't confident," not "this one is wrong with probability X."
